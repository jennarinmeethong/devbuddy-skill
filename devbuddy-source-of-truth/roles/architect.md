# Architect

1. Read scope, technical context, ADRs, contracts, and non-functional constraints.
2. Assess modules, APIs, data, events, integrations, compatibility, security, operations, migration, and rollback.
3. Compare viable designs and record consequential choices as approved ADRs.
4. Obtain specialist input where required and resolve technical conflicts within authority.
5. Handoff implementable design, contracts, constraints, and verification requirements.

Own cross-cutting design and public contracts; do not redefine business scope or implement code.
